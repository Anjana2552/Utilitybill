## Introduction

The **Utility Bill Management System** is a comprehensive mobile application designed to help users efficiently manage and track their utility bills across multiple providers. Whether you're paying for electricity, water, gas, WiFi, DTH (Direct-to-Home), or other utilities, this application provides a centralized platform to organize, monitor, and manage all your bills in one place.

### Key Benefits

- **Centralized Bill Management:** Track all utility bills in one application
- **Bill Status Tracking:** Monitor payment status and due dates
- **Payment History:** Maintain a complete record of all payments
- **Notifications:** Receive timely reminders for upcoming bills
- **Digital Records:** Store bills and receipts digitally
- **Reviews & Feedback:** Share experiences with utility providers
- **Complaint Management:** Report issues to utility authorities directly through the app

## Getting Started

### System Requirements

- **Mobile Device:** iOS 12+ or Android 6+
- **Internet Connection:** Required for all operations
- **Storage:** Minimum 100MB free space

### Installation

1. **Download the App:**

1. **Login:**

## Detailed Feature Guides

### 1. Managing Bills

#### Adding a New Bill

1. From Dashboard, tap **"+ Add Bill"** or go to Bills Section
2. Select **Utility Type** (Electricity, Water, Gas, WiFi, DTH, Other)
3. Enter **Provider Name** (e.g., "State Electricity Board")
4. Enter **Consumer Number** (from your bill/letter):
   - Electricity: Consumer Number
   - Water: Connection Number
   - Gas: Consumer ID
   - WiFi: Subscriber ID
   - DTH: Subscriber ID
5. (Optional) Select plan/package name
6. Tap **"Save Bill"**

#### Viewing Bill Details

1. Go to **Bills** section
2. Tap on any bill card
3. View:
   - Consumer details
   - Reading information
   - Amount details
   - Due date
   - Current status

#### Editing Bill Information

1. Open bill details
2. Tap **"Edit"** button
3. Update necessary information
4. Tap **"Save Changes"**

#### Deleting a Bill

1. Open bill details
2. Tap **"Delete"**
3. Confirm deletion

### 2. Payment Management

#### Making a Payment

1. Go to **Payments** section
2. Tap **"New Payment"** or select a bill
3. Choose **Payment Method:**
   - Credit Card
   - Debit Card
   - Bank Transfer
   - Online Payment Gateway
   - Cash (if applicable)
4. Enter **Payment Amount**
5. For Card Payments:
   - Enter card number, expiry, CVV
   - Select preferred bank (if offered)
6. Review payment details
7. Tap **"Confirm Payment"**
8. Wait for confirmation (typically instant)

#### Viewing Payment History

1. Go to **Payments** section
2. See all past payments in chronological order
3. Tap any payment to view:
   - Payment date and time
   - Amount paid
   - Method used
   - Reference number
   - Receipt

#### Adding Payment Methods

1. Go to **Profile** → **Payment Methods**
2. Tap **"+ Add New Method"**
3. Select method type
4. Enter details:
   - **Card:** Card number, name, expiry, CVV
   - **Bank:** Bank name, account number, IFSC
5. Save method

#### Managing Saved Payment Methods

1. Go to **Payment Methods**
2. **To Edit:** Tap method → Edit details → Save
3. **To Delete:** Tap method → Delete → Confirm
4. **To Set Default:** Tap method → "Set as Default"

### 3. Notifications

#### Notification Types

1. **Bill Notifications:** New bill generated
2. **Payment Alerts:** Payment received, receipt available
3. **Due Date Reminders:** 7 days before due date
4. **Status Updates:** Bill status changes
5. **Broadcast Alerts:** Important system announcements

#### Managing Notifications

**Viewing:**
- Tap **Notifications** tab
- See all recent notifications

**Marking as Read:**
- Tap notification to view details
- Automatically marked as read

**Deleting:**
- Swipe left on notification
- Tap Delete

**Notification Settings:**
1. Go to **Profile** → **Notification Preferences**
2. Toggle notification types:
   - Bill Notifications
   - Payment Reminders
   - Promotional Offers
   - Authority Updates
3. Set time preferences

### 4. Reviews & Ratings

#### Writing a Review

1. Go to **Bills** section
2. Select a bill for that provider
3. Tap **"Leave Review"**
4. Rate provider (1-5 stars):
   - 5 stars: Excellent service
   - 4 stars: Good service
   - 3 stars: Average service
   - 2 stars: Poor service
   - 1 star: Terrible service
5. Write comments (optional but recommended)
6. Submit review

#### Viewing Provider Reviews

1. Open bill details
2. Tap **"Provider Details"**
3. See average rating and review count
4. Read recent reviews from other users
5. Check overall health score

### 5. Complaint Management

#### Filing a Complaint

1. Go to **Bills** section
2. Tap on relevant bill
3. Tap **"File Complaint"**
4. Select **Issue Category:**
   - Billing Error
   - Wrong Amount Charged
   - Late Bill Generation
   - Service Interruption
   - Meter Reading Issue
   - Connection Issues
   - Other
5. Provide **Complaint Description:**
   - Be specific and detailed
   - Include relevant bill references
   - Attach screenshot/image if applicable
6. Submit complaint

#### Tracking Complaint Status

1. Go to **Complaints** section
2. View all your filed complaints
3. Tap complaint to see:
   - Current status (Open/In Progress/Resolved)
   - Complaint ID (for reference)
   - Authority response
   - Expected resolution date
4. Add follow-up comments
5. Mark as resolved when issue is addressed

### 6. Account Management

#### Updating Profile Information

1. Tap **Profile** icon
2. Tap **"Edit Profile"**
3. Update:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Phone Number
   - House Number
   - Address
4. Tap **"Save Changes"**

#### Changing Password

1. Go to **Profile** → **Security**
2. Tap **"Change Password"**
3. Enter **Current Password**
4. Enter **New Password** (8+ characters)
5. Confirm new password
6. Save changes

#### Setting Up Biometric Login

1. Go to **Profile** → **Security**
2. Enable **"Biometric Login"**
3. Follow device prompts to register

#### Managing Email Preferences

1. Go to **Profile** → **Email Preferences**
2. Toggle:
   - Bill Emails
   - Payment Confirmations
   - Promotional Emails
   - Newsletter

#### Account Security

- **Two-Factor Authentication:** Enable OTP verification on login
- **Login History:** View recent login locations and times
- **Device Management:** See and manage connected devices

### 7. Bill Analytics & Reports

#### Viewing Bill History

1. Go to **Bills** section
2. Tap **"History"**
3. Filter by:
   - Utility type
   - Date range
   - Status
4. Export as PDF (if needed)

#### Understanding Bill Charts

- **Bar Chart:** Comparison of monthly amounts
- **Line Chart:** Trend over time
- **Pie Chart:** Distribution across utilities

#### Monthly Usage Report

1. Go to **Analytics**
2. Select month and year
3. View:
   - Total bills
   - Total paid
   - Outstanding amount
   - Payment methods used

## Utility Types Supported

### 1. **Electricity (Power Supply)**
- **Information Needed:** Consumer Number, Meter Number
- **Readings:** kWh (units consumed)
- **Frequency:** Monthly billing
- **Typical Amount:** ₹500 - ₹2000+ (varies)

### 2. **Water Supply**
- **Information Needed:** Connection Number
- **Readings:** Liters/Cubic Meters
- **Frequency:** Monthly/Quarterly
- **Typical Amount:** ₹200 - ₹800

### 3. **Gas (LPG/Natural Gas)**
- **Information Needed:** Consumer ID or Connection Number
- **Readings:** Cubic Meters/Cylinders
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Typical Amount:** ₹300 - ₹1000

### 4. **WiFi/Internet**
- **Information Needed:** Subscriber ID / Account Number
- **Plan Info:** Plan name and speed
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Typical Amount:** ₹300 - ₹1200

### 5. **DTH (Direct-to-Home Television)**
- **Information Needed:** Subscriber ID
- **Plan Info:** Channel package selected
- **Frequency:** Monthly/Quarterly
- **Typical Amount:** ₹200 - ₹500

### 6. **Other**
- **For:** Any other utilities not listed above
- **Information:** As per the provider's requirement

## Payment Management

### Accepted Payment Methods

1. **Credit Card**
   - Visa, MasterCard, AMEX
   - Security: 3D Secure enabled
   - Billing address verification required

2. **Debit Card**
   - All major Indian banks
   - Security: OTP authentication
   - PIN or 3D Secure

3. **Bank Transfer**
   - NEFT/RTGS/IMPS
   - UPI supported
   - Instant settlement

4. **Online Payment Gateways**
   - Razorpay
   - PayPal (where available)

5. **Cash Payments**
   - At designated payment centers
   - Available for select providers

### Payment Security

- **SSL Encryption:** All transactions encrypted
- **PCI DSS Compliance:** Card data protection
- **OTP Verification:** Two-factor authentication
- **Secure Gateways:** Trusted payment processors
- **Fraud Detection:** Real-time monitoring

### Invoice & Receipt

After successful payment:

1. **Automatic Email:** Receipt sent to registered email
2. **In-App Receipt:** Available in Payment History
3. **Download PDF:** Printable receipt available
4. **Reference Number:** Unique transaction reference for queries

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

### General Questions

**Q1: How do I reset my password?**
- On login screen, tap "Forgot Password"
- Enter your registered email
- Check email for reset link
- Set new password

**Q2: Can I use multiple accounts?**
- No. Each email can have only one main account
- But one account can manage multiple bills
- You can list authorized family members

**Q3: Is my data secure?**
- Yes. All data encrypted with HTTPS
- Passwords hashed using bcrypt
- PCI-DSS compliant for payment data
- Regular security audits conducted

**Q4: What is OTP verification?**
- One-Time Password sent to your email
- Required for certain actions
- Valid for 10 minutes
- Unique for each action

### Billing Issues

**Q5: Why is my old bill showing?**
- Check "Show Archived Bills" option
- Or filter by date range
- Contact provider if bill details seem incorrect

**Q6: I entered the wrong consumer number. What now?**
- Edit the bill (from bill details page)
- Update the consumer number
- Save changes

**Q7: My bill shows "Pending" but I've paid it.**
- May take 24-48 hours to update
- Payment must be from registered method
- Check payment reference on receipt
- Contact customer support if delayed

### Payment Issues

**Q8: Payment failed but debit card has been charged.**
- Check email for detailed error message
- Verify if amount was actually deducted
- If deducted: contact your bank
- Refund usually processed within 5-7 business days

**Q9: I don't have a saved card but payment failed.**
- Ensure card details are correct
- Check card expiry date
- Verify billing address matches bank records
- Try different browser or device

**Q10: Can I cancel a payment?**
- Only if not yet processed
- Select payment from history
- If "Pending" status, click "Cancel"
- Already processed payments cannot be reversed

### Technical Issues

**Q11: App keeps crashing.**
- Update app to latest version
- Clear app cache (Settings → Apps → Clear Cache)
- Uninstall and reinstall app
- Ensure minimum 100MB free storage

**Q12: Can't login - keeps saying wrong password.**
- Ensure Caps Lock is OFF
- Try resetting password
- Check Num Lock (if using desktop)
- Try different WiFi/mobile network

**Q13: Notifications not appearing.**
- Check notification settings
- Ensure notifications enabled for the app
- Check device notification settings
- Verify WiFi/mobile data is enabled

**Q14: Bills not syncing / loading slowly.**
- Check internet connection (WiFi/4G preferred)
- Try force refresh (pull down)
- Restart the app
- Restart your phone if issue persists

### Account Issues

**Q15: How do I delete my account?**
- Go to Profile → Account Settings
- Tap "Delete Account"
- Confirm by entering password
- Account deleted within 30 days
- **Warning:** All data will be permanently deleted

**Q16: Can I transfer my bills to another account?**
- No direct transfer available
- Must add bills to new account separately
- Contact support for bulk migration assistance

## Best Practices

### Security

✅ **Do:**
- Change password every 3 months
- Use strong passwords (8+ characters with numbers)
- Enable biometric login
- Logout from shared devices
- Review login history regularly

❌ **Don't:**
- Share password with anyone
- Use same password elsewhere
- Click links in emails asking for password
- Connect via unsecured WiFi
- Keep sensitive info in notes

### Billing Management

✅ **Do:**
- Add bills as soon as you recite them
- Enable payment reminders
- Set up auto-pay for regular bills
- Keep receipt records
- Review bills monthly

❌ **Don't:**
- Miss due dates intentionally
- Ignore late payment charges
- Share consumer numbers publicly
- Assume bill generation is automatic
- Ignore complaint responses
